## Questions for you

1. **Ambient light.** The usual approach (Unity, Godot, Unreal) is that ambient light has no position: it is an *environment/scene setting* (color or sky-based). The "sun" is a **directional light**, where only rotation matters and the editor draws its arrow wherever the entity sits. Recommendation: move ambient into Environment settings, add a default Sun (directional) linked to day/night, and drop the ambient-light gizmo. OK?
2. **Physics selection in New Project:** offer Bullet / Simple (both exist), or also plan a new backend (e.g. Jolt)?
3. **Video and asset sources:** downloading from YouTube/Instagram and pulling from Mixamo can break those platforms' terms. Recommendation: local files plus "import from URL" only for direct-download links; YouTube/Instagram/Mixamo stay out unless you accept that risk. OK?
4. **Big features:** OK to keep section 4 at "design note first" and decide on each one after reading the note?
