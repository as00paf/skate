# Manual test: GPL-03 / GPL-11 / GPL-12 (skate prefabs, mount toggle, board alignment)

Every run must start from a **new project**. Do not reuse a project created before these changes: old scenes
keep the old skater spawn height, floor position and board collider.

Prerequisites: Windows, a keyboard, and optionally an XInput gamepad. Menu and button names below are as in the
editor; if a label differs slightly, use the closest entry.

## A. Setup

1. Open a terminal in the worktree root and run `.\gradlew.bat run`.
   Expected: the Skate editor window opens without errors in the console.
2. Create a NEW project with the project wizard (File menu, or the "New Project" button on the start screen).
   Use a new empty folder and the name `GPL-Test-<date>`. Confirm.
   Expected: an empty project opens with a default scene.
3. Open the Asset Browser and switch to the **Prefabs** tab.
   Expected: entries for Floor, Skateboard and Skater are listed.
4. Spawn **Floor** (double-click or the spawn button on the entry).
   Expected: a flat 20 m x 20 m slab appears. In the inspector its Transform is position (0, -0.1, 0),
   scale (10, 0.1, 10), so its top surface is at y = 0.
5. Spawn **Skateboard**.
   Expected: a board about 0.8 m long appears. Transform scale is 0.0017 on all axes. Its long axis runs along
   Z (blue gizmo axis) and its width along X. It is not a small speck and not huge.
6. Spawn **Skater**.
   Expected: a character about 1.8 m tall appears. Transform position y is 0.15. Move it with the gizmo to
   about (3, 0.15, 0) so it stands about 3 m from the board.

## B. Mesh / collider / rigid body alignment (GPL-03, GPL-12)

7. Turn on physics debug draw with the "Toggle Physics Debug" command (command palette or Edit/Debug menu).
   Expected: collider wireframes become visible in the viewport.
8. Zoom in on the Skateboard.
   Expected: the box wireframe is 0.2 m wide (X) by about 0.76 m long (Z) and about 0.1 m high. It sits on the
   floor (bottom edge at y = 0), is centred on the deck and has the same orientation as the mesh. It is NOT a long
   strip along X, not floating above the board and not buried in the floor.
9. Zoom in on the Skater.
   Expected: the capsule/box wireframe wraps the whole body from the feet (y about 0) to the head (about 1.82 m).
   It does not extend below the floor.
10. Press Play (the "Play" toolbar button).
    Expected: the board drops a few centimetres at most and rests on the floor flat, without tilting, spinning
    or sinking. The skater stands still in the idle pose with the feet on the floor.
11. With the game running, look at the board from the side.
    Expected: the wheels touch the floor and the collider wireframe still matches the mesh, with no visible
    offset between them.

## C. Walking (GPL-01/02)

12. Hold W (or push the left stick forward). Camera and movement are camera relative.
    Expected: the skater walks forward; holding Shift (or the left trigger) makes it run. Releasing stops it.
13. Walk past the board and walk over it.
    Expected: the board does not move and the skater is not blocked or pushed by it (characters ignore the board
    collider layer).

## D. Mounting (GPL-09 / GPL-11)

14. Stand more than 1.5 m away from the board and press **E** (keyboard) or **gamepad Y**.
    Expected: nothing happens. The skater keeps walking and the board does not move.
15. Walk to within 1.5 m of the board and press **E** (or **Y**).
    Expected: the board jumps under the skater's feet, aligned with the skater's heading (the board's long axis
    points where the skater faces). The skater switches to the skate controller (no more walk/run animations).
16. Push forward (W, or hold Shift, or the left stick forward).
    Expected: the skater pushes and accelerates, the board travels with the skater and stays under the feet
    with no sideways drift or offset. It does not fall off or lag behind.
17. Steer with A/D (or the left stick left/right) while rolling.
    Expected: the skater and board turn together; the board's heading always matches the skater's.
18. Press Space (or gamepad A) while rolling.
    Expected: an ollie: the skater and board leave the ground together and land back together, still aligned.
19. Hold Left Ctrl (or LB) at speed.
    Expected: manual: the balance mechanic starts. Releasing returns to riding.
20. Stop (release the stick, brake with S) and press **E** (or **Y**) again.
    Expected: the skater returns to walking and the board stays exactly where it was left, resting on the floor.
21. Walk away from the board, then press **E** (or **Y**) out of range.
    Expected: nothing happens (range check). Walk back within 1.5 m and press it: mounting works again.
22. (Gamepad) repeat steps 14 to 21 using only the gamepad: left stick = move, Y = toggle mount, A = jump/ollie,
    LB = manual.
    Expected: identical results to the keyboard.
23. Open Settings > Input (rebinding) and rebind "toggle_mount".
    Expected: the new key or button toggles mounting and E/Y no longer do.

## E. Camera presets (GPL-11)

24. While mounting and dismounting, watch the camera.
    Expected (current state): the camera does not change. `GameCamera` is not yet constructed by the engine, so
    the walk/ride presets (4 m / 1.4 m and 6 m / 1.0 m) are not applied visually. The hook
    `PlayerMotionSystem.onCameraPreset` fires on each mode change and is covered by unit tests only. Reporting
    this as a failure is not needed; it is a known gap awaiting a camera follower.

## F. Automated checks

25. Run `.\gradlew.bat test --tests "com.pafoid.skate.game.prefabs.SkateboardAlignmentTest"`.
    Expected: PASSED (collider fits inside the mesh bounds of `assets/obj/skateboard_free_model.glb`, bottom at
    y = 0, long axis Z).
26. Run `.\gradlew.bat test --tests "com.pafoid.skate.game.player.MountTest"`.
    Expected: all PASSED (range check, mount/dismount transitions, camera presets, collision masks, E/Y defaults).
27. Run `.\gradlew.bat test`.
    Expected: BUILD SUCCESSFUL.

## Known limitations

- The camera preset is not visible yet (step 24).
- Grinds are stubbed: there is no rail detection yet, so the grind state never triggers.
