# GPL-21: skateboarding control schemes

Controls for each scheme are in `docs/controls.md`. Use a **new** project, so the MainScene HUD has the trick text. A gamepad is best; every step also works on the keyboard (IJKL is the right stick).

1. Create a new project, press **Play**, walk to the board and press **E**. Default scheme **EA Skate**: pull the right stick back and flick it up → ollie. Back then up-left → the board rolls over (**Kickflip** on the HUD); up-right → **Heelflip**. Each landing adds to the score.
2. Still in EA Skate: from the back, sweep the stick round half a circle to the top → the board turns 180° flat (**Shove-it**). Sweep a full circle → **360 Shove-it**. The board spins through each trick before the landing.
3. Press **Esc**. The menu shows **Controls: EA Skate** under Language. Click it → **Controls: Session**, then resume. Back foot down (right stick) + front foot up (left stick) → ollie; front foot up-left or up-right → kickflip or heelflip; back-foot half or full circle → shove-it or 360 shove-it. The front foot also steers.
4. **Esc** → click until **Controls: Skater XL**. Steer with **LT/RT**, push with **A**; tricks as in Session.
5. **Esc** → **Controls: THPS**. **A** ollies; **X** + left / right / down / up → kickflip / heelflip / shove-it / 360 shove-it. Ollie, then press **X** + right in the air → it becomes a heelflip. Ollie and press **X** + up just before touching down → bail (the board did not come round).
6. With the pause menu open while riding, confirm a menu item with gamepad **A**: it works (riding no longer hides menu input). Get off the board: Shift sprints, Space jumps, in every scheme.
7. Stop Play, close and reopen the project, press Play: **Esc** still shows **Controls: THPS**. The French language entry shows **Commandes : THPS**.
8. Export the game and run it: the scheme saved there starts as chosen, and steps 1 and 5 behave the same.
