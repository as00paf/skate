# QA-13 manual test: Feature Showcase play smoke test

QA-13 adds an automated test. These steps check by hand what it checks, from a **new** project.

1. Run `.\gradlew.bat test --tests "com.pafoid.skate.game.samples.FeatureShowcaseSmokeTest"`. Every `Showcase_*` test passes, except `Showcase_Audio_BothSourcesStartPlayingInPlay` until AUD-09 merges and `Showcase_Joints_SliderBlockDropsToItsLowerLimit` until BUG-019 is fixed.
2. Start the editor and choose **New Project**. Switch to the `FeatureShowcase` scene tab, which was loaded from the saved file.
3. Press **Play**. Particles: fire, smoke and fountain keep emitting, and the sparks burst once.
4. Physics: the box stack and the balls fall and come to rest on the floor or the banks; nothing sinks through. Vehicle: the car drops onto its four wheels and stops bouncing.
5. Joints: the red hinge pendulum and the blue cone-twist lamp swing. The slider block drops 2 m along its rail and stops (fails until BUG-019 is fixed).
6. Animation: the character walks in place. Audio: the tone plays and gets louder as you fly toward the cyan sphere (needs AUD-09).
7. Click each legend button (Overview and every area). The camera flies to that area each time.
8. Negative check: in `FeatureShowcase.kt`, set the Fountain's `emissionRate = 0f` and re-run step 1. The particles test fails and names `[Particles] Fountain`. Revert the change.
