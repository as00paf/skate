# Editor Settings

Editor Settings holds your personal preferences: how the editor looks and behaves on your computer. They do not change the game, and they are not shared with other people who work on the project.

## What it is

The window has a list of categories on the left and their settings on the right:

- **General**: the editor language, and whether the last project opens on startup.
- **Interface**: theme (dark, light or high contrast), UI scale, tooltips, unit system (metric or imperial) and the gamepad overlay.
- **Display**: V-Sync, fullscreen and anti-aliasing (MSAA) for the editor window. Some of these need a restart.
- **Graphics**: overall quality, shadows, textures and MSAA of the editor's 3D view.
- **Scene view**: gizmo snapping steps, editor camera speed and mouse sensitivity.
- **Script Editor**: when code completion appears.
- **Auto Save**: save your settings automatically every few minutes.

A search box at the top finds a setting by name. Each setting has a short description below it.

## How to use it

1. Open it from **Settings > Editor Settings...**.
2. Click a category on the left, or type in the search box.
3. Change values. A star and **Unsaved changes** show that something is not saved yet.
4. Click **Apply** to save and keep the window open, **OK** to save and close, or **Cancel** to throw your changes away.
5. Use **Restore to default** to put a setting back as it was.

Keyboard shortcuts are not changed here: the **Key Bindings** button opens the [Key Bindings](keybindings.md) window.
