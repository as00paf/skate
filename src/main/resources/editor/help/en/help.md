# Help

The Help window explains the editor. Every window has an article here: what the window is for and how to use it. Two general topics, [Getting started](getting_started.md) and [Keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md), are not tied to a window.

## Opening it

- Click the **?** button at the top right of any window to read that window's article.
- Right-click a window's tab and choose **Help**: same result.
- Open **Help > Help Window…** in the menu to see the list of every topic.
- **Help > Getting Started** and **Help > Keyboard Shortcuts** open those two topics.

## How to use it

1. Pick a topic in the list on the left. Type in the search box above it to keep only the topics whose title contains your words.
2. Read the article on the right. Underlined words are links: click a link to another topic to open it here, or a web address to open it in your browser.
3. Use the **back** and **forward** arrows at the top to return to the topics you read before. The **list** button clears the article and shows the topic list alone.
4. Below the article, the **Open** buttons show the window the article is about, and the windows that work with it.

The articles follow the editor's language. When an article is not translated yet, the English one is shown with a short note.

The Help window docks like any other window. Close it with the X on its tab and reopen it from **View > Windows > Help** or the **?** of any window.
