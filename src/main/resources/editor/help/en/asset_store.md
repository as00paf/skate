# Asset Store

The Asset Store brings free 3D content into your project: models, textures, materials and environment images. You do not need to make every rock and wall yourself.

## What it is

The store gathers several free libraries:

- **Poly Haven** and **ambientCG**: the editor downloads the files for you.
- **Kenney** and **Quaternius**: curated packs. You download the zip from their website, and the editor imports it.

Free does not always mean "no conditions". Each item has a **license** that says how you may use it and whether you must credit the author. The store records the license and author of everything you import in the project's **attribution** list.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Browse** tab. Choose a **Source** and a **Type**, then type a word in the search box and press **Enter**.
2. Click an item to see its preview, author and license.
3. Click **Import**. The files are copied into `Assets/Store/<source>/<item>/` and appear in the [Asset Browser](asset_browser.md). **Ctrl+Z** removes the import.
4. For Kenney and Quaternius, open the pack's web page, download the zip, then use **Import downloaded pack...** and choose the zip.
5. Open the **Attribution** tab to see what your game must credit. Copy it into your game's credits before you publish.
