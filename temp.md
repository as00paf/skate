Icon design 

Revised App Icon Design:

* Shape: Rounded square.
* Central Element: A top-down view of a foot (minimalist skate shoe) on a skateboard. The skateboard deck will have a subtle, abstract graphic, emphasizing user control and simulation.
* Color Palette (Chill Aesthetic & Minimalism):
    * Primary (Background): Soft, muted "Misty Grey" or "Pale Sage Green" (#CFD8DC or #D4E4D4).
    * Secondary (Skateboard Deck): Desaturated "Dusty Rose" or "Warm Beige" (#E0BBE4 or #F7E6C4).
    * Accent (Foot/Shoe Outline): Light "Off-White" or "Light Grey" (#F8F8F8 or #EAEAEA) for subtle detail.
    * No strong contrasts or neon.
* Minimalism: Clean lines, simple shapes, focusing on clarity and a relaxed feel.

This aims for a sophisticated, calm, and inviting look, reflecting the simulation's realism from the player's perspective.
